The Forge

Born from Fire

Iron Souls started with a welder, a pile of scrap, and a simple idea: metal has memory. Rebar that once held up buildings. Bolts that held engines together. Nuts that kept machines running.

Each sculpture is built from reclaimed materials, giving new life to metal that has already lived. The figures are not just decorative objects. They carry weight, history, and character.

Based in Stellenbosch, Western Cape, we create collector pieces, functional art (bottle holders, pot stands), and custom commissions. If you can imagine it, we can forge it.

  • 100% handcrafted, no moulds or casting
  • Reclaimed and new steel hardware
  • Custom commissions welcomed
  • Delivery available across South Africa
